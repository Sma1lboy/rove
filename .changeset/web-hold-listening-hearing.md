Web, on a phone: while you hold to talk, the bubble shows only what is being said (your voice as a waveform, then the words) and the control under it shows the time and where to slide. After you let go the words grey out while they are finished, the control counts the wait with three moving dots, and the bubble no longer dims or slips behind the strip over the terminal.

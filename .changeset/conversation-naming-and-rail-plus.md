A new conversation in a project no longer shows the title of another conversation in the same folder before you type anything: a conversation that shares its folder is now named from its own first message. The web rail has a `+` in its header that opens a Scratch conversation in one click, and each project's `+` now shows all the time instead of only on hover. Deleting says what it deletes: the menu reads "Delete conversation" or "Delete task", and the confirm explains what happens to the tabs and the folder. Dialog titles keep your own words as written instead of uppercasing them.

[web] [ios] When the phone can't reach your computer, the screen no longer offers only Retry: it also has "switch computer or pair again" (in the iPhone app) and a Details section with a Copy button that shows the relay, the start of the pairing code, the error, when it failed and when this device last connected. The iPhone app's own "can't reach" screen has the same Details and Copy. When the iPhone app opens through the relay, the centred `[ rove ]` now glides to the list's wordmark at the top left while the page fades in, or just fades with Reduce Motion on. With the keyboard up, the key row now sits directly on the keyboard.

Settings → Voice in the web app is one choice now: Dictation off, on this Mac, or a cloud endpoint, with only what that choice needs shown under it. Off hides the mic and stops the sidecar. The local model can be uninstalled, which frees its roughly 4 GB, and a saved cloud endpoint can be forgotten along with its key; the endpoint is otherwise kept when you switch away, so switching back needs nothing retyped.

[web] [ios] On a phone, a session's PR chip sits at the row's right, beside its status, and is its own tap target: a tap presses it and raises a sheet with each PR's title, state, checks and an Open on GitHub button, without opening the session. A row whose second line held only the PR is now one line. Tapping outside any sheet closes it without also tapping what is underneath.

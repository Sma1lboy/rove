On a phone, the web client's text is one step larger: lists and hints 13px, body text 15px, session titles 16px, rows 44px tall, and the terminal's font 14px (45 columns on a 390px screen). The controls under a session — attach, the message field or hold-to-talk, send, and the key row — and the view switch in the session's top bar are now glass buttons in the style of iOS: see-through, blurred, with a lit top edge. Back and close are round glass buttons with only a chevron or ×, at the top left of every phone page, Settings and dialog, with the page's title beside them. The session list has a floating bar the list scrolls under: a gear for Settings at the bottom left and a larger, accent New task button at the bottom right, in place of the New task row at the top. The key row shows six keys a thumb wide (esc, ⇧tab, ^C, ↑, ↓, ⏎) and keeps Ctrl, tab, ← and → behind ⋯. They turn solid when the system asks for less transparency. The desktop web and the terminal app are unchanged.

On a phone, the web client's text is one step larger: lists and hints 13px, body text 15px, session titles 16px, rows 44px tall, and the terminal's font 14px (45 columns on a 390px screen). The controls under a session — attach, the message field or hold-to-talk, send, and the key row — and the view switch in the session's top bar are now glass buttons in the style of iOS: see-through, blurred, with a lit top edge. Back and close are round glass buttons with only a chevron or ×, at the top left of every phone page, Settings and dialog, with the page's title beside them. They turn solid when the system asks for less transparency. The desktop web and the terminal app are unchanged.

On a phone, the web client's text is one step larger: lists and hints 14px, body text 16px, session titles 17px, session rows 52px tall with larger status marks, buttons 44px with 22px icons, and the terminal's font 14px (45 columns on a 390px screen). The controls under a session — attach, the message field or hold-to-talk, send, and the key row — are glass in the style of iOS: see-through, blurred, with a lit top edge. Buttons are rounded squares like Send: back and close at the top left of every phone page, Settings and dialog, the inbox tray with its count badge, and the session list's floating bar, with a gear for Settings at the bottom left and a solid accent New task of the same size at the bottom right. A session's top bar has one view button showing the view on screen; tapping it opens a sheet of Terminal, Context, Files, Diff and Browser, and the session's title gets the room. The key row shows six keys a thumb wide (esc, ⇧tab, ^C, ↑, ↓, ⏎) and keeps Ctrl, tab, ← and → behind ⋯. Under a session, attach, the message field, the mic and Send share one capsule, and Send turns solid accent when there is something to send; no mic is shown where the browser cannot record. A photo dragged from another app onto the message field or the New session box attaches to the message, with "Drop to attach" while it is held over; a photo pasted into the session's message field attaches there instead of being typed into the terminal as a path. Settings is a grouped list with plain glyphs; a setting's name and control share a line with its note under them, and choices open a sheet from the bottom. On New session the project, engine and model chips sit above the prompt box, so the box rests on the keyboard. Dialogs sit low, above the keyboard, with two equal buttons. Sheets and dialogs slide up and back down, a session or Settings slides in from the right, and buttons give a little when pressed; nothing moves when the system asks for reduced motion, and the glass turns solid when it asks for less transparency. The desktop web and the terminal app are unchanged.

[web][ios] On a phone the session list reads like the old iPhone app's: each row starts with its engine's mark, shows its branch and how far it is behind (`↓126`) under the title, and at the right its status in a word (`needs you`, `running`, `done`, `error`) over its age. New task is a bar along the bottom beside Settings and Inbox, and says whether it starts in a worktree or the folder. Project headers show a task count and a `⋯` that opens the project's actions. Routines use the same rows (engine mark, or a terminal for a script; `ok`, `failed`, `paused` over when it last ran), and a tap opens the routine in a sheet. New routine and Kanban's New issue are the same bar. The list no longer starts with Kanban, Routines, Issues and Mods as rows of text: two buttons beside `[ rove ]` open them, Routines (with a count when a routine needs you) and Extensions, which opens a sheet listing Kanban, Issues and your mod panes, with Add extension at the bottom.

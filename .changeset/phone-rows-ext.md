[web][ios] On a phone the session list reads like the old iPhone app's: each row starts with its status mark, shows its branch and how far it is behind (`↓126`) under the title, and its age and engine at the right. Project headers show a task count and a `⋯` that opens the project's actions. Routines use the same rows, and a tap opens the routine in a sheet. The list no longer starts with Kanban, Routines, Issues and Mods as rows of text: two buttons beside `[ rove ]` open them, Routines (with a count when a routine needs you) and Extensions (Kanban, Issues, mod panes).

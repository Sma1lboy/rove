`rove --web` has the Worktrees page (`x` in the Tasks list): every worktree of every saved project with the TUI's badges, `d` to delete (with the force confirm for dirty ones) and `l` to land. In the workspace, Ctrl+1..9 and Ctrl+[ / Ctrl+] switch tabs, Ctrl+E opens the new-conversation picker and F2 renames the tab.

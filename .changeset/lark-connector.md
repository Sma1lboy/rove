Lark (飞书) is a built-in chat connector. `rove lark connect` sets up lark-cli's app if there is none, checks its bot and allows you; after that, DM the bot or @ it in a group to talk to your own agent, which answers in the thread and starts tasks for real work. Each message gets OnIt, then DONE when the agent's turn ends. The agent sits at the bottom of the sidebar, and Settings → Connectors → Chat shows Lark beside WeChat.

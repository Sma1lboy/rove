`rove --web` now has the TUI's prefix keys: press Ctrl+A anywhere, including in the terminal, then `n` new task, `,` settings, `i` Inbox, `h`/`l` move between panes, `z` Zen, `p` ask for a PR, `w` close the tab. A hint panel lists the options while the prefix is armed. Dialogs opened from the terminal now close with Esc.

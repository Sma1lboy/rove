Resizing the task rail is now findable: grab the workspace pane's left border line — the divider you can already see — and drag. Over that line, and for the whole drag, the mouse pointer turns into a four-way move arrow. Before, the drag handle sat on the blank column just left of the line and nothing marked it, so the resize looked like it didn't exist. Nothing new is drawn, and terminals that don't let an app set the pointer shape keep their usual arrow; the line still drags there.

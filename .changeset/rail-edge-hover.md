Resizing the task rail is now findable: grab the workspace pane's left border line — the divider you can already see — and drag. While the cursor is on that line, and for the whole drag, the line turns dotted (`⋮`) in its own color, and terminals that let an app set the pointer shape also show a four-way move arrow. Before, the drag handle sat on the blank column just left of the line and nothing marked it, so the resize looked like it didn't exist. At rest the rail looks exactly as it did.

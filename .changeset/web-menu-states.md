The browser client's right-click menu behaves like a native one. It flips to the pointer's other side instead of sliding over the row you clicked, and highlights nothing until you point at an entry or press a key; Home and End jump to the ends. **Delete** sits below a divider and fills red when highlighted, and the row you right-clicked stays highlighted while its menu is open. Hover and list highlights across the rail, tabs, palette and settings now use a neutral wash, which leaves the accent colour for selection and focus. Menus and popovers get a softer two-layer shadow and open with a short fade.

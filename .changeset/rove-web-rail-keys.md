`rove --web` now takes the TUI's Tasks-list keys while the list has focus: `j`/`k`, `gg`/`G`, Enter, `→` into the terminal, `/` to filter, `t` to change the sort, `n` for a new task, and `r`, `b`, `v`, `Shift+P`, `d` on the task under the cursor. The right-click menu shows each entry's key.

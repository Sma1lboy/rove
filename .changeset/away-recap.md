When the web page comes back after more than 10 minutes away (a wake, a phone resume, a reconnect), the top of the rail shows a one-line recap: `since 01:20 · 3 finished · 1 needs you · 1 failed · 2 merged`. Each count opens those tasks (needs you opens the Inbox), the × dismisses it, and the last look is kept per browser.

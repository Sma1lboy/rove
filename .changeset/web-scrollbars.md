The browser client (`rove --web`) draws thin scrollbars that match the rest of the page: a faint rounded thumb on a transparent track, no arrow buttons, brightening when you hover or drag it, in the light and dark halves of every theme. Lists such as the Tasks rail and Files keep the scrollbar's lane, so rows no longer shift sideways when the thumb appears, and the terminal's slider is rounded without its dark inset shadow.

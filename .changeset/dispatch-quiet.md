The web Dispatch page no longer draws an ASCII stick-figure courier. A prompt in flight is one dot riding its wire; once it lands, its state shows on the target node as a glyph and a word (delivered, working, needs you, done, failed), and a prompt waiting on you gets an amber outline. Nodes and the hub lose their boxes, wires get thinner, and nothing animates once no prompt is travelling.

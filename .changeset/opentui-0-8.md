Rove's terminal interface now runs on OpenTUI 0.8.4, up from 0.4.3. What you may notice: a dialog's scrollbar thumb now shows how much of the dialog is in view (it used to stay short), a wide character in the title of a working pane takes the border's flowing colours like the rest of the title, and a terminal pane appears one frame later after it opens. A text field still shows the start of its value until the text is longer than the field.

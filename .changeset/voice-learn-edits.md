Web: dictation learns from your fixes. On the phone bar, a term you correct inside a take before sending ("Jithub" → GitHub) is remembered and rewritten in the next takes; a toast says what was learned and offers `[ undo ]`. Punctuation, sentence-case capitals, plain English words and rewrites never count. `"learn": false` in `~/.rove/voice/config.json` turns it off.

The browser client's right-click menu is grouped instead of one long list. Opening work, editing the task, its branch, copying and Delete each sit in their own section, with a divider between them. **Set status ›** and **Copy ›** open their choices to the side, with the current status checked, so a status is one click instead of a dialog. Arrow keys (or `h`/`l`) and Enter move in and out of these choices, and Esc backs out one level at a time. A menu opened while the terminal had focus now takes the keys itself, where before an arrow or Enter could reach the shell underneath.

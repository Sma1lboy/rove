Zen mode is gone. The TUI has a `Files` button at the workspace's top right that shows or hides the Files pane, and `ctrl+a` `z` does the same; the pane is hidden until you open it and stays as you left it. The `ZEN` chip at the foot of the task list and the "Start in zen mode" setting are removed, in the TUI and on the web. On the web, `ctrl+a` `z` now toggles the side panel's Files column, as its top-right button does.

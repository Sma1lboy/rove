The sidebar row menu now carries the three verbs its keyboard already had: **Open in editor** (`o`), **Rename branch** (`b`), and **Change engine** (`v`). Each acts on the row you right-clicked, and the engine entry opens a picker over your available engines instead of cycling blindly. Rename branch is left out on rows with no branch of their own, such as a project's main checkout.

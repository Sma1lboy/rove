[web] [ios] Text fields grow a line at a time as you type and shrink back as you delete, with a short height animation, then scroll inside once they reach about 240px (half the space the keyboard leaves on a short screen). This covers the message bar under a session, the New session prompt, and every multi-line field in dialogs, the issue drawer, review notes, feedback and snippets, on the phone and on desktop. The message bar grows upward over the terminal, so the bar and the key row stay put.

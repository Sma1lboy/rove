Drag the task rail's right edge to set its width. The edge lights up under the cursor, double-clicking it returns to the width Rove picks from your terminal, and a width you drag to is remembered across restarts. It is an override rather than a replacement: a terminal too narrow to honour it squeezes the rail down to what fits and pays the full width back when the window grows again.

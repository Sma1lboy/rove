[web][ios] On a phone, every menu rises from the bottom as a sheet: a session's or project's long-press menu, a tab's menu, Kanban's and the diff base's drop-downs, and New session's project, branch, engine and model chips. An entry with choices (Set status, Copy, More) opens them in the same sheet, and Cancel sits last. The Inbox is a sheet too, one session per row with its state and age: tap a row to open the session, swipe it left to ack it, long-press it to reply, open or ack, and Ack all sits at the bottom. In the session list, swipe a row left to archive it or right to pin it.

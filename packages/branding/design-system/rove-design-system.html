Brand book

Rove is a local-first workspace for running many AI coding sessions at once. It has two clients that draw the same facts: a terminal UI (OpenTUI + React) and a web client (React + Tailwind). Both paint from one theme. The look is a quiet, warm-neutral workbench with one terracotta accent, monospace for structure and sans for prose. The tokens here are the default claude theme. Dark is the default theme and comes first.

Content fundamentals

  • Sentence case for actions and titles: "New task", "Run again", "Delete". No Title Case, and no ALL CAPS except the rare kicker. Placeholders and empty-list notes are lowercase, terminal style: "no tasks yet", "no pending attention".
  • Name the thing the user does. An action keeps one name through the whole flow: the button says "Delete", the toast says "Deleted".
  • Short and literal. No marketing verbs (unlock, seamless, supercharge), no filler, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
  • Errors say what happened and what to do next. Never just "Error" or "Something went wrong". Show the error in the block that failed, not as a page banner.
  • Use real ellipses. Loading…, Saving…, never ....
  • Unknown is not empty. Before the daemon's first reply, show nothing or a placeholder at full height. Never "No tasks". An empty state names the next step: "no tasks yet", then "New task above: pick a project folder, then say what to do".
  • The TUI's words win. Web strings reuse the TUI's message catalogue, and every new string ships in en and zh.

Visual foundations

Colour

  • Ground: paper for the page and terminal, surface for anything raised (dialogs, menus), inset for an active tab, a field or a toast, and menu for a selected or cursor row, one step deeper, so a selected row never looks like a hovered one.
  • Text: ink > muted > subtle, in that order of importance. Set body in ink, secondary lines in muted, and project heads, ages, placeholders and key legends in subtle.
  • One accent, used for meaning. accent (terracotta) marks selection, focus, the wordmark brackets, running or unseen status and the primary action. Never use it for hover: hover is the neutral hover wash.
  • Status tones are fixed. warning (amber) means a wait you can clear: needs input, rate limited. error (red) means broken until you act. accent means running or unseen, and subtle means idle. Every status also has a glyph, because colour is never the only signal (see StatusGlyph).
  • Washes: accent-soft for a picked chip or a row mid-drag, hover for pointer hover and menu highlight.
  • Lines: one hairline weight in line, with line-active for a focused field. Use space to separate groups before you reach for a line.
  • Never hard-code a hex in a component. prefs-theme.ts remaps every token from the active TUI theme at runtime, so a raw colour goes stale when the theme changes.

Type

  • Two faces, two jobs. mono carries structure: the wordmark, project heads, chips, glyphs, tabs, keys, paths, IDs and code. sans carries prose: body, dialog copy, settings descriptions and tab-strip titles. Don't spread mono into paragraphs, and don't put sans into a glyph column, because alignment depends on 1ch.
  • Four sizes only: fs-meta 12, fs-body 13, fs-title 14 and fs-display 16.
  • Show hierarchy with weight and tone, not new sizes: 700 for the wordmark, 600 for project heads, tabs and dialog titles, 500 for kickers, 400 elsewhere. Dialog titles use fs-title.
  • Never change weight on hover or selection, because bold widens the label and shifts the row. Mark state with inset, hover or colour.
  • Figures are tabular (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on body), so counts and versions don't jitter.

Space, radius, layout

  • The rhythm is small: space-0.5 through space-5 (2–20px). A list row is 26px tall (40px on touch) with space-0.75 × space-2 padding. Dialogs pad space-4.5 × space-5. Don't add a value 1px off an existing one.
  • Radii: radius-sm (6) for controls and rows, radius (8) for dialogs and menus, radius-xs (4) for a small control inside a row, radius-full for the switch and pills. A row inside a padded panel takes the concentric inner radius, calc(var(--radius) - <padding>), never a literal.
  • Panes share width by ratio (flex-grow), not pixels. The web app is a grid: rail minmax(220px, 280px), workspace, and a Files panel at clamp(300px, 28vw, 440px). Below 560px (70 columns × 8px) it shows one pane at a time with a back bar, like the TUI's narrow mode.
  • One left edge and one right edge per column. Text-bearing flex children get min-width: 0 and truncate with an ellipsis.

Elevation and borders

  • Only floating things cast a shadow: shadow-popover for menus and tips, shadow-dialog for dialogs. Each also has its own line hairline border. Resting surfaces have neither.
  • A box is a solid hairline or nothing. No dashed borders, no cards inside cards, no coloured side stripes, no gradients, glows or glass.

Motion

  • Motion answers an action: a press (scale(0.97) on :active), an open or a close. Nothing animates on mount or on a theme switch.
  • Overlays fade in over 140ms or rise 4px over 180ms with cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), and sink back out over 140ms. Menus scale from 0.96 over 120ms. The rail folds over 200–220ms with cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1).
  • Animate only transform and opacity. Gate everything on prefers-reduced-motion.

States and interaction

  • Every control has rest, hover, focus-visible, active and disabled states. Disabled is 50% opacity and says why in a title or hint.
  • Focus is 1px solid focus with a 1px offset. A field marks focus on its own border with line-active, because a ring would be clipped inside a scroller.
  • Hit targets are at least 24×24px with a pointer and at least 40–44px tall on touch. Small controls grow an invisible hit area on coarse pointers.
  • Anything revealed on hover is also reachable from the keyboard, a right-click menu and touch.

Iconography

  • The status vocabulary is text glyphs, not an icon font: ○ idle, ● unseen, ? needs input, ! attention, ◷ rate limited, † dead, ✓/✗ checks, ◇ subagents, ☁ another machine. A braille spinner (⠋⠙⠹⠸⠼⠴⠦⠧⠇⠏) marks a running row. Users can switch to the starburst or ascii sets.
  • The web draws a running or waiting status as a 3×3 dot orb, stepped on a shared 80ms clock, in the same tone.
  • Engine status lines and prompt themes use Nerd Font symbols. The web ships them as the "Rove Nerd Symbols" face (src/fonts/symbols-nerd-font-mono.woff2, limited to the private-use ranges), so viewers need nothing installed.
  • The only drawn glyph is the thin chevron (10×10 viewBox, 1.3 stroke, round caps), which points down and rotates a quarter turn for "right".
  • No emoji as icons.

Brand

  • Wordmark: [ rove ] in wordmark style: mono 14px 700, tracking 0.02em, brackets in accent, name in ink. It is always text, never an image.
  • Mark: a bracketed capital R, in terracotta brackets and a paper-white R on the dark ground (assets/Logos/). Use the image file; never redraw it.

Not synced

  • Font files are not in fonts/. The web bundles JetBrains Mono through @fontsource/jetbrains-mono (400, 500, 600 and 700) and the "Rove Nerd Symbols" face (1.2 MB, over the 1 MB per-file cap); load JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts or the package when consuming this system.
  • Components are static renditions hand-written from packages/rove-web/src/ui/* (Tailwind recipes rewritten as plain CSS in components/bundle.css). The React library itself was not built or bundled.
  • The marketing palette in packages/branding/src/colors.ts (porcelain light #f6f3ec, used for videos and the landing page) differs from the app's light theme. It is not included as tokens.
  • Other bundled TUI themes (conductor, skylight, tokyonight) are not included. Only claude is.

Terminal UI

The TUI is the product, and the web client follows it. Both clients read one theme file (an opencode-format JSON under packages/rove/src/tui/context/theme/) with a dark and a light half. The TUI paints the slots directly. The web maps them onto its tokens (prefs-theme.ts). Theme mode auto follows the terminal's or the browser's colour scheme.

Frames

  • Every framed surface is a rounded box: spread FRAME ({ border: true, borderStyle: "rounded" }) and set borderColor={theme.border}. A bare border falls back to OpenTUI's square style.
  • Layout is Yoga flexbox. Panes share width with flexGrow. Fixed width/height is for a documented convention, a fixed glyph or a modal only. Avoid height="100%".
  • Below about 70 columns the TUI goes into narrow mode and shows one pane at a time. The web matches this at 560px.

Keys

Every new or moved chord needs the owner's sign-off. Ship it as PROPOSED and record it in docs/design/keybinding-decisions.md. The web binds the same keys as the TUI wherever the browser allows. Key legends are mono, subtle, and hidden on touch screens.

Components

ButtonActions

Button

The one action control: a filled terracotta button for the primary action, an accent text link for everything else.

Static rendition hand-written from packages/rove-web/src/ui/button.tsx.

Variants

  • filled (buttonClass()): accent fill, paper label, radius-sm, meta size, 600 weight, 6×14px padding. Use at most one per view, for the thing the view is for ("Create task", "Start").
  • danger: the same button in error, for a confirmed destructive step ("Delete").
  • box: the Engines page's smaller one (5×12px, 500 weight).
  • link (default): accent text that underlines on hover. Use for secondary actions ("Create PR", toggle scope) and trailing actions in a row.
  • floating: an action raised over content (a preview's "ask in chat" beside a selection). Mono, surface ground, line-active hairline, shadow-popover. The caller places it.
  • row / card: a whole list row or a pickable tile. The caller paints them.

Rules

  • A press scales to 0.97 (off under reduced motion). Disabled is 50% opacity and should say why in a title.
  • hit: on touch, link grows an invisible target (roomy 12px above and below and 6px each side, or tight in a wrapped row of links).
  • Don't override what the variant sets (position, colour, padding). There is no class merging, so stylesheet order would decide.
  • Label: verb first, sentence case.

Wordmark

The bracket wordmark [ rove ]: always set as text, never as an image.

Static rendition hand-written from WORDMARK and BRACKET in packages/rove-web/src/ui/shell.ts, as used in rail-view.tsx.

ChipPicker

SwitchInputs

Switch

An on/off toggle: a 30×18px inset track with a 12px muted knob. When on, the track turns accent and the knob turns paper.

Static rendition hand-written from packages/rove-web/src/ui/switch.tsx.

  • Renders a role="switch" button, so on is announced as aria-checked. The consumer supplies on, onClick and an aria-label. There is no visible label inside.
  • Use it in Settings rows for a preference that applies at once. A value with more than two states is a cycling row with a chevron, not a switch.
  • Track and knob move over 120ms, with no motion under reduced motion. flex: none keeps its size beside a long label.
TextFieldInputs

TextField

A labelled single-line field: a kicker over a mono input over a hint, stacked with a 6px gap.

Static rendition hand-written from INPUT, HINT and KICKER in packages/rove-web/src/ui/text.ts, and FIELD in ui/form.ts.

  • Input: paper ground, line hairline, radius-sm, mono fs-meta, 8×10px padding. Focus moves the border to line-active. It draws no outline ring, because a ring would be clipped inside a scrolling dialog body.
  • Kicker: sans 12px, 500 weight, in muted. Hint: sans 12px in subtle.

  • A textarea adds TEXTAREA (min-height 72px, line-height 1.45, max 50vh). On touch, every field is 16px so iOS doesn't zoom.
RowLists

Row

The list row used by rail tasks, tab rows, files, dialog choices and inbox cards. It is a full-width button: one line, title truncating, chips at the right edge.

Static rendition hand-written from rowClass() in packages/rove-web/src/ui/row.ts and the rail recipes in ui/rail.ts.

  • Size: 26px minimum height (40px on touch), 3×8px padding, 8px gap, radius-sm. Sizes file (24px, mono meta) and pane (22px) exist for denser lists.
  • States: rest has no ground, hover gets the hover wash, selected sits on menu (one step past inset, so it never matches a hover), and moving (move mode or mid-drag) gets accent-soft. A press scales to 0.97.
  • Title (ROW_TITLE): min-width: 0, flex 1, ellipsis. A rail task title is fs-title (14px). A cut-off title shows in full as a row tip beside it.
  • Status glyphs appear only on tab rows (a 1ch mono glyph column, indented 6px). Task rows start flush with their project head.
  • Project heads are mono 12px, 600 weight, in subtle, with no divider rule. A + appears on hover.
  • A drag shows the rows already in the order the drop would leave them, never an insertion line.

DialogOverlays

Dialog

A modal on a scrim: title row, body, then trailing actions.

Static rendition hand-written from OVERLAY, dialogClass, DIALOG_* and ACTIONS in packages/rove-web/src/ui/overlay.ts and ui/text.ts.

  • Panel: surface, line hairline, radius, shadow-dialog, 18×20px padding, 12px between blocks. It is 440px wide, or 620px (wide) for a two-column form, and sits 12vh from the top.
  • Title: sans fs-title (14px), 600 weight, ink, sentence case. It often quotes the user's own words and is never an uppercase kicker.
  • Close: a 24px × in muted. Esc closes too. Focus is trapped inside and returns to where the dialog was opened from.
  • Actions sit right-aligned with a 14px gap: the link actions first, the filled action last. They stay sticky while the body scrolls.
  • An aside inside the body (a delete warning, a brief) is an INSET_BOX on paper, never a nested card.
  • It rises 4px in over 180ms and sinks out over 140ms.
MenuOverlays

Menu

The right-click and dropdown menu: a surface panel with items that pair a label with its key.

Static rendition hand-written from menuClass, menuItemClass, menuKeyClass and MENU_SEP in packages/rove-web/src/ui/overlay.ts.

  • Panel: radius, line hairline, 4px padding, shadow-popover, minimum width 200px (160px for a submenu). It opens at the pointer, or at the target for Shift+F10, and scales in from 0.96 over 120ms.

Toast

A one-line notice, stacked bottom right, that clears when clicked.

Static rendition hand-written from TOASTS and toastClass(error) in packages/rove-web/src/ui/overlay.ts.

StatusGlyph

The shared status vocabulary: one glyph and one tone per daemon row status, drawn the same way by the TUI sidebar, its tab strip and the web rail.

Static rendition hand-written from packages/rove/src/tui/lib/glyphs.ts and packages/rove/src/lib/row-status-glyph.ts. The web orb is ui/status-dots.tsx. Its frames here are illustrative, because the real ones come from dot-levels.ts on an 80ms clock.

  • The consumer supplies the daemon's RowStatus. Never derive a status, count or label in a client.
  • Tones: accent means running, busy or unseen. warning means needs input or rate limited. error means an error, a dead engine or a failed delete. Idle is subtle (the orb uses muted).
  • Glyph sets: braille (default, one cell wide), starburst (✻ ✧ ✦ ❢, opt-in) and ascii (7-bit). Spinner frames never reuse a settled glyph.
  • Other marks: ✓/✗ checks, ◷ rate limited, † dead, ↻ routine failed, ◇N running subagents, ≠ conflict, ☁ another machine.
  • Glyphs sit in a 1ch mono column, so every row's glyph shares one centre.

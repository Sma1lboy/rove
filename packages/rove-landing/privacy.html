Privacy

The Rove iPhone app opens the Rove page served by your own computer. It has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing to the people who make Rove.

Where your data goes

The app talks only to the computer you paired it with, over your local network, Tailscale, or a tunnel you set up yourself, such as a Cloudflare Tunnel or a relay on your own Cloudflare account. Nothing passes through a server run by Rove. What you type, the tasks you open and the terminal output you read travel between your phone and your computer and nowhere else.

The coding agents on your computer may send your prompts to the AI providers you set them up with. That happens on your computer, under those providers' own policies, not through the app.

What stays on your phone

  • The pairing link of each computer, with its token, kept in the iOS Keychain. forget on the computers screen deletes one.
  • What the Rove page remembers between visits, such as the token it signs in with, the last task you opened and which panels were open, in the app's web storage.

Camera and microphone

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code your computer shows. Nothing is recorded or kept. Pasting the link works without it.
  • Microphone: records only while you hold the talk button. The audio goes to your computer, which turns it into text. If you set your computer up to use a speech-to-text service, your computer sends the audio there.

try a demo runs on sample data built into the app. It makes no network requests and saves nothing.

Questions

The app is a developer tool and is not aimed at children. If this page changes, its history is public in the repository. Questions go to GitHub issues.
